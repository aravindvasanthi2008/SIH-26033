## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
 
  #AgriConnect

AgriConnect is a smart agricultural platform that connects farmers with nearby buyers based on crop availability and expected demand. Farmers can enter their harvest quantity and expected harvest date. The system predicts demand and matches farmers with suitable buyers before harvesting. It also supports group orders and nearby delivery planning. This helps farmers reduce unsold crops, earn better prices, and deliver fresh products to customers at affordable prices.

## Feasibility and Viability


1. Feasibility of the Idea

AgriConnect can be developed using Python, Django, HTML, CSS, and a database. It can initially be tested with local farmers and nearby buyers at a low cost.

2. Potential Challenges and Risks

- Limited internet access in rural areas.
- Delivery delays and transportation costs.
- Changes in crop prices and demand.
- Building trust between farmers and buyers.

3. Strategies for Overcoming Challenges

- Develop a simple, mobile-friendly website.
- Partner with local transport providers.
- Update product prices regularly.
- Provide verified farmer profiles and order tracking.

## Impact and Benefits


#1. Potential Impact on the Target Audience

AgriConnect helps farmers connect directly with consumers, retailers, and wholesale buyers. It improves market access for farmers and makes fresh agricultural products more accessible to customers.

#2. Benefits of the Solution

- Economic: Better earnings for farmers and affordable prices for consumers.
- Social: Supports rural communities and local farmers.
- Environmental: Reduces food waste through demand prediction.
- Practical: Provides easy ordering, price comparison, and delivery tracking.

## Research and References


Research Work:

Existing digital agriculture platforms show that technology can help farmers access markets, compare prices, and connect with buyers. AgriConnect builds on this idea by adding harvest-based buyer matching, demand prediction, and delivery planning.
